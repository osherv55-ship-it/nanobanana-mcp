## Line 2 (PRIMARY GOAL) — Aesthetics Academy

### Product Overview
**One-liner:** In-person academy that trains doctors and nurses in aesthetic injections — and in building an aesthetics business.
**What it does:** Hands-on (frontal) injection courses (botox, fillers) for licensed medical professionals, plus business-of-aesthetics training: how to open, run, and grow an aesthetics practice.
**Product category:** Professional medical-aesthetics training / CME-style courses.
**Product type:** Education — in-person cohort courses.
**Business model:** Course fees per cohort. ⚠️ price range TBD.

### Target Audience
**Who:** Physicians and nurses — (a) clinicians entering aesthetics for the first time, (b) practicing injectors upskilling, (c) clinicians who can inject but don't know how to build the business side.
**Primary use case:** "Get me safely and credibly into aesthetic practice — with real hands-on training, not slides."
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Give me hands-on injection skills I can use on real patients the week after."
- "Teach me the business: pricing, patient flow, marketing, opening a clinic."
- "Give me a credential and mentor network so I'm not alone after the course."

### Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Doctors/nurses want into aesthetics (high income, quality of life) but most courses are theory-heavy with little supervised hands-on practice, and none teach how to actually get patients afterwards.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of injecting unsupervised for the first time; fear of investing in a course and ending up with a certificate but no patients.

### Differentiation
- **Hands-on, frontal training** with real practice — not a Zoom slideshow.
- **Business-of-aesthetics track** — the only academy whose operator also runs patient-acquisition marketing for working clinics; graduates learn the exact playbook used on real campaigns.
- **Graduate flywheel:** finish the course → open a practice → the marketing arm can launch your patient acquisition (before/after content, testimonial videos, Meta/Google Ads).
- Taught with actively practicing doctors — real cases, real results (see clinic roster below).

### Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "There are cheaper courses" | Cheaper courses sell theory; here you practice hands-on and leave with a business plan + marketing playbook. |
| "Will I really get patients after?" | The operator runs patient acquisition for 8+ working doctors; graduates get the same machine. |
| "I don't have time for a long program" | Frontal, concentrated format built for working clinicians. ⚠️ confirm schedule format. |

**Anti-persona:** Non-licensed practitioners looking for medical-injection training.

### Goals
**Business goal:** Fill the next cohort (primary company-wide goal right now).
**Conversion action:** Course registration / discovery call / open-day signup. ⚠️ confirm the exact funnel step used today.

## Line 1 — Marketing for Aesthetic-Medicine Clinics

### Product Overview
**One-liner:** Done-for-you content and paid-ads engine for aesthetic-medicine doctors and clinics in Israel.
**What it does:** Produces the full marketing asset stack for injectors/clinics — before/after collages in a consistent clinic template, cleaned and subtitled doctor testimonial/promo videos, and runs the Meta + Google Ads campaigns behind them. Heavily automated via an in-house AI pipeline (image generation/editing, video cleanup, multi-language subtitles).
**Product category:** Medical-aesthetics marketing agency / clinic growth service.
**Product type:** Service, billed **per project / per campaign**.

### Target Audience
**Target companies:** Aesthetic-medicine clinics and independent injector doctors in Israel (lip fillers, botox, skin treatments). Current roster includes doctors: Rami, Rawan, Fadi, Gilad, Sharon, Tomer, Yasmin, Etty.
**Decision-makers:** The doctor-owner (usually also the clinician), sometimes a clinic manager.
**Primary use case:** A steady flow of new aesthetic patients (leads/bookings) without the doctor spending time on content or ads.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Fill my appointment book with quality aesthetic patients."
- "Make me look premium and trustworthy online — like the top-tier clinics."
- "Take content off my plate: I film once, you turn it into everything."

### Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Doctors are excellent clinicians but have no time/skill for content and ads; generic marketing agencies don't understand medical aesthetics (visual standards, trust dynamics, regulatory sensitivities around medical advertising).
**Why alternatives fall short:** Generic agencies produce off-brand creatives, don't know how to present before/after results credibly, and can't produce clinical content in Hebrew at a fast cadence.
**What it costs them:** Empty slots, price-shopping patients, dependence on word-of-mouth only.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of looking "cheap" or unprofessional; anxiety about medical-board/advertising compliance; being invisible next to competitors.

### Differentiation
- **AI-powered production pipeline** — before/after collages auto-composed in the clinic's design template (single-frontal, anchored to facial landmarks, lips-only crops, serif stacked template); interview videos auto-cleaned (fillers, stutters, pauses) and subtitled in Hebrew/English/Arabic; turnaround in hours, not weeks.
- **Aesthetics-only specialization** — the field's visual conventions are built into the tooling.
- **Full stack under one roof** — content + Meta Ads + Google Ads run by the same operator, so creative learns from campaign data.
- Hebrew-first with Arabic/English reach — matches the real patient mix in Israel.

### Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already get patients from word of mouth" | Word of mouth caps out and doesn't fill new-treatment capacity; ads compound it. |
| "Marketing agencies burned me before" | Aesthetics-only; show the doctor-roster work (before/after + video samples) in the first meeting. |
| "Is this compliant / will it look tacky?" | Clinic-template system keeps everything premium and consistent; medical-advertising sensitivity is a core competency. ⚠️ confirm exact compliance stance. |

**Anti-persona:** Clinics wanting one-off cheap flyers; businesses outside medical aesthetics.

## Shared

### Customer Language
**Words to use:** תוצאות לפני/אחרי, מראה טבעי, תור מלא, מטופלות/מטופלים, קליניקה, הזרקות, אסתטיקה רפואית, פרימיום, אמינות; academy: הכשרה מעשית, האנדס-און, ליווי, קהילת בוגרים.
**Words to avoid:** "זול", "מבצע לוהט", exaggerated medical claims, anything implying guaranteed results. ⚠️ extend from real customer conversations.

### Brand Voice
**Tone:** Premium, calm, trustworthy, medical-grade — never salesy-loud.
**Personality:** מקצועי, אסתטי, מדויק, דיסקרטי.

### Proof Points
- Roster of 8+ doctors with produced campaigns (before/after series, testimonial videos).
- In-house tech: automated collage agent, doctor-video pipeline with multi-language subtitles, multimodal asset memory, evidence tooling (PubMed / clinical-trials search) for research-backed content.
- ⚠️ add real numbers when available: leads/month per clinic, cost per lead, cohort sizes, graduate outcomes.

### Synergy Between the Two Lines
- Academy graduates become clinic-marketing clients (built-in pipeline).
- Clinic results (before/after, testimonial videos) are the academy's best proof and ad creative.
- Research-backed content (PubMed citations) serves both: patient trust for clinics, professional authority for the academy.

### Goals (Overall)
**Primary business goal:** Fill the next academy cohort.
**Secondary:** Per-project clinic campaigns (content + ads) for the doctor roster and new clinics.
**Conversion actions:** Academy — registration/open-day signup; Clinics — booked discovery call / WhatsApp lead.
