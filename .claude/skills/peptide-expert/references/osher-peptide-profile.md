## Responder phenotype: FAST / STRONG RESPONDER (confirmed)
Reacts fast and strongly to peptides — documented for **retatrutide, MOTS-c, and KPV**. This is a
consistent cross-peptide pattern (not formally a named phenotype in the literature, but real for her).
**Implication: always start at the LOWEST end, one peptide at a time, titrate slowly, expect systemic

### KPV first-dose event (for reference)
2 units (0.1 mg, from a 10 mg vial + 2 mL bacteriostatic water = 5 mg/mL; so 1 unit = 50 µg),
subQ, evening. Within 15–45 min: tingling/warmth in hands, "lighter"/vitality feeling, brief
tinnitus flicker, transient right-temple pressure, "whole body at higher intensity," and a pleasant
sensation replacing pain in normally-painful areas. All **mild, wavelike, transient — resolved, felt
well.** Interpreted as a benign vasomotor/autonomic (MC1R→cAMP→NO vasodilation) + early nociceptor
modulation response — a real early peripheral effect, not the (slower) anti-inflammatory action, and
not merely placebo. Confirms she is a strong responder → hold at 2 units ≥1 week, single peptide.

## Reconstitution reference (her vials)
- **KPV 10 mg + 2 mL = 5 mg/mL → 1 unit = 50 µg.** Her dose 0.15 mg = 3 units (NOT 5). 2 units = 0.1 mg
  (her cautious start). 5 units = 0.25 mg.
- **Retatrutide:** reconstituted with 1 mL (two 0.5 mL syringes of water). 10 units = vial_mg × 0.1 mg
  — VERIFY vial mg (10 mg vial → 10 u = 1 mg). She reacts strongly to Reta → low & slow.
- **MOTS-c:** her "5 units = 0.05 mg" implies 1 mg/mL — VERIFY vial mg + water. 0.05 mg = 50 µg is very
  low (good for her sensitivity).
- **The #1 error:** dose is undefined without the water volume. Always confirm before computing units.

## Safety triage taught (for real-time symptom events)
Benign: mild, fluctuating, fading, no progression. STOP + seek care (m*d, 101/ER) if: sudden severe
headache, vision/speech/one-sided weakness, lip/throat swelling, breathing difficulty/chest tightness,
hives/flushing spreading, fainting/racing heart, persistent/worsening tinnitus + hearing loss/vertigo,
or rapid escalation. "Better to call and hear it's nothing." Unapproved substance + strong responder =
low threshold to contact a clinician.
