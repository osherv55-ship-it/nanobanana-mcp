# MCAS / histamine support toolkit — tailored to Osher

Built from a sonar-deep-research run (50 searches) + last30days community, filtered through her profile.
Everything here is **supportive** — not a validated cure. Evidence tiers: A human-RCT / B human-small or
strong-mechanism / C animal-cell / D anecdotal. **Two hard rules: one new thing at a time on a calm day,
lowest dose; and always a CLEAN single-ingredient formulation (no citric acid, no "natural flavors", no dyes).**

| Fexofenadine (Telfast, H1) | blocks histamine | base layer; time it 1–2 h BEFORE a known trigger. A 2nd dose is safe for an active late-phase wave (wide margin). [A] |

Quercetin is prophylactic (below), not an acute rescue — it works taken BEFORE a trigger, not during.

## 🗓️ DAILY baseline kit — "start here" order
1. **Telfast** daily (base). H2 (famotidine) for gut histamine is recommended in MCAS but **only after a cardiac
   check** — mild negative inotropy could bother her low BP. [A/B]
2. **Vitamin D — dose to a blood level** (hers was low at 21); mast-cell stabilizing via VDR. Keep the K2. Don't
   megadose blindly (toxicity). [B/C]
3. **Quercetin** — mast-cell stabilizer, **best taken ~30–60 min BEFORE a predictable trigger**. Start 125–250 mg,
   clean capsule (many contain citrus bioflavonoids/flavors — avoid). [C human mast cells + pilot]
   - Luteolin (liposomal) = broader cytokine coverage, more experimental; add only if quercetin is tolerated. [C]
4. **Magnesium glycinate** nightly — headache prevention, sleep, PMDD, muscle. Low BP → start low. [B]
5. **Zinc-L-carnosine + modest L-glutamine** (she owns both) — gut barrier → less antigen load on gut mast cells. [B/C]
6. **DAO** situationally before higher-histamine meals. [B]
7. **Low-histamine diet + nervous-system regulation + sleep + moderate exercise** — the non-supplement foundation. [A/C]

Secondary/later (after the core is stable): omega-3 (mainly for Lp(a)/CV, unclear MCAS benefit [B]), B-complex with
**modest** B6 ≤25 mg as a DAO cofactor (ties to her low folate; check B12/iron too — B12 came up in community as a
frequent "root cause") [C], PEA (animal mast-cell data) [C], Perilla (has human anti-allergy data but **lowers BP** →
risky for her) [C].

## ⛔ AVOID (for her specifically)
- **Immune STIMULANTS** — spirulina, chlorella, echinacea, elderberry, medicinal mushrooms (reishi/cordyceps). They
  rev the immune system / raise the same cytokines that made her feel fluey; she needs calming, not activation. [C/D]
- **Mangosteen / α-mangostin** — pro-inflammatory in some human cells, unpredictable. [C]
- **Magnesium citrate / oxide** — citrate (her trigger) + laxative; oxide laxative. Glycinate instead.
- **Anything with citric acid, "natural flavors", synthetic dyes** — her confirmed excipient triggers.
- **Strongly hypotensive botanicals** (holy basil, moringa, high-dose nettle) — her BP is already 94/52.
- **NSAIDs and Optalgin/dipyrone** for pain — dipyrone is a leading drug cause of anaphylaxis + cross-reacts with
  NSAID hypersensitivity; both can worsen a mast-cell flare. Acetaminophen is the safe analgesic.
