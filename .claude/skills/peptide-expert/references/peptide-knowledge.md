# Peptide knowledge base

Accumulated research (mechanism from `sonar-deep-research`, community reactions from
`last30days` + `sonar-pro`). **All entries: not FDA-approved for these uses unless noted;
human data thin; anecdote ≠ evidence. Always re-run live research for anything time-sensitive
(FDA status, "last 30 days" sentiment) — this file is a starting map, not the final word.**

Evidence tiers used below: **A** = human RCT · **B** = human non-RCT / small trials ·
**C** = animal/preclinical · **D** = anecdotal/community only.

## Regulatory anchor (as of research in 2026)
FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) met **July 23–24, 2026** on 503A bulk
listing. Panel backed 6 of 7: **BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, MOTS-c, Semax, Epitalon cleared;
Emideltide voted down.** Advisory only — does **not** equal FDA drug approval. Community
noted many yes-voters had peptide-industry ties.

## KPV (Lys-Pro-Val)
- **What:** C-terminal tripeptide of α-MSH. Anti-inflammatory.
- **Mechanism (C):** enters cells via **PepT1** transporter (upregulated in *inflamed* tissue) →
  inhibits **NF-κB** and MAP-kinase → cuts pro-inflammatory cytokines 40–65% in tissue models.
  NOT melanocortin-receptor mediated. No pigment/hormonal effects.
- **Uses discussed:** gut (IBD/colitis/IBS), skin, wound healing, systemic inflammation, MCAS.
- **Human evidence:** none (D for humans; C in mice, anchored on 2008 Dalmasso study ~205 µg/kg).
- **Routes → goal (key insight):** **oral for gut** (PepT1 in inflamed intestine — the only
  route with a gut-targeting mechanism); **subQ for systemic/joints/widespread skin**;
  **topical 0.1–0.5% for local skin**; nasal for sinus; rectal (sparse) for distal colitis.
- **Injection site myth:** subQ is absorbed **systemically regardless of site**. Injecting the
  abdomen "over the gut" does NOT gut-target (belly fat doesn't drain to intestine); injecting
  "near the painful joint/lesion" has **no proven local advantage** — plausible only for
  superficial skin. Practitioners inject abdomen/thigh for convenience + rotation.
- **Dosing (community D):** oral 200–500 µg/day (up to 1 mg in flare); subQ 200–500 µg 1–2×/day
  (~95–100% bioavailability vs ~40–60% oral). Oral empty-stomach ~30 min pre-food for systemic;
  with food to keep it luminal for gut. subQ 29–31G, 45–90°, no aspiration, rotate sites.
- **Timeline (community D):** gut/inflammation fast (1–2 wk); skin/hair/joint slow (6–8 wk, then
  compounds). ~1/3 report nothing (sometimes bunk product). Most positive reports **stack KPV
  with BPC-157**, rarely KPV alone.
- **Side effects:** injection-site redness/itch, mild GI upset (oral), mild headache, occasional
  short-lived fatigue (minority, first days). "Herx" claims usually mislabeled — a real
  Herxheimer is microbial die-off, not "I felt off." Early fatigue in CIRS user reported.
- **KPV + retatrutide:** active community question (r/Retatrutide, Jul 2026), NO settled protocol,
  no interaction data. KPV appears in stacks alongside Reta users (framed as heal/anti-inflam
  support), not as a proven combo. Reta's own GI side effects are the loose rationale.
- **Injection food timing:** for **subQ KPV, food timing is irrelevant** (systemic; the empty-
  stomach/PepT1 rule is ORAL-only). No evidence-based "wait X hours" rule for injection — pick a
  fixed convenient time (AM). "Inject fasted" is protocol convention/anecdote, not proven.
- **Post-injection gut symptoms — what to reach for (general info, D):** simethicone (Gas-X) =
  safest for gas/bloating; peppermint oil (Colpermin) helps IBS cramping but worsens reflux;
  **AVOID ibuprofen/Advil (NSAID) for gut symptoms — irritates gut lining, can worsen inflammation /
  trigger IBD flares.** Acetaminophen gentler if analgesic needed. Persistent/worsening/fever/blood → MD.

## Semax (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro)
- **What:** Russian synthetic ACTH(4-10) analog + Pro-Gly-Pro tail. Nootropic + neuroprotective.
  Non-corticotropic (does NOT raise cortisol).
- **Mechanism (C, strong):** upregulates **BDNF/NGF** + TrkB in hippocampus (the core, best-proven
  action → plasticity, memory, neuron survival); modulates serotonin turnover; **potentiates
  dopamine release only under stimulant challenge** (amphetamine/methylphenidate); inhibits
  enkephalin-degrading enzymes (opioid tone); shifts immune/inflammatory gene expression.
- **Evidence by use:** ischemic stroke **B** (Russian human trials, ↑plasma BDNF, faster
  recovery) — strongest. Cognition/Alzheimer's **C** (mice) + pilot humans. ADHD/attention
  **C/theoretical** (no human RCT). Mood/anxiety **C** (weak; Selank stronger for anxiety).
  Healthy nootropic **D** (anecdote; PeptideBase aggregate ~38% improved / 25% mixed /
  33% no effect / 4% worse).
- **Forms & potency:** standard Semax (0.1%) < **N-Acetyl Semax** (~3–5× potent) <
  **Adamax / NA-Semax Amidate** (most potent + longest).
- **Dosing (community D):** DAILY, 1–2×, MORNING (short half-life 1–2 h; weekly dosing is
  wrong for Semax — that's CJC-1295 logic). Standard 100–600 µg/dose (start 100–200);
  NA-Semax 50–200; Adamax 50–150. Intranasal: 0.1% = 1 mg/mL → 1 drop ≈ 50 µg, 1 spray ≈ 100 µg.
  Cycle 10–14 days on / 7–14 off (tolerance/desensitization). Stop dosing by ~2–4 PM.
- **Side effects:** nasal irritation (most common), headache, **insomnia/overstimulation**
  (Semax is stimulating — opposite of sedating). **Fatigue** = uncommon/paradoxical: usually
  dose-too-high rebound, sleep disruption from late dosing, or individual sensitivity → fix by
  lowering dose + morning-only. Anxiety/restlessness in sensitive users.
- **Interaction flag:** Semax **amplifies stimulants** (amphetamine/methylphenidate) — real
  theoretical concern for anyone on ADHD stimulant meds (more effect AND more side effects).
- **Selank** (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro): sister peptide, GABA_A allosteric modulator,
  anxiolytic without benzo sedation. Better human anxiety evidence than Semax.

## Perimenopause / hormonal-balance candidates
No single peptide "balances hormones." Best-per-goal:
- **Kisspeptin-10 (B, experimental):** KISS1R/GPR54 on GnRH neurons → GnRH pulses → LH/FSH.
  Most mechanistically aligned with the reproductive axis. BUT effect is **estradiol-context
  dependent**: in hypoestrogenic (low-estrogen) state it can paradoxically **suppress** LH; with
  estradiol present it amplifies. In perimenopause estrogen swings → unpredictable. Amplifies
  hormonal *fluctuation* → high-risk for PMDD. Not approved.
- **PT-141 / Bremelanotide (A):** MC3R/MC4R → central dopamine → sexual desire. **Only peptide
  here with real RCTs in women; FDA-approved (Vyleesi) for premenopausal HSDD.** Hormone-neutral
  (doesn't touch estrogen/LH). Best for libido, not "hormone balance." Side effects: nausea,
  flushing, **transient BP rise** (caution with cardiovascular risk / high Lp(a)).
- **MOTS-c (C, strong preclinical):** mitochondrial peptide → AMPK → inhibits osteoclasts
  (OPG/RANKL) + muscle/metabolic. Best preclinical case for **bone/metabolic** protection in
  ovariectomy (estrogen-deficient) models. No human trials. Hormone-neutral.
- **GH axis (Ipamorelin/CJC-1295/Sermorelin) (C/B):** GHRH-R/GHSR → GH/IGF-1. Body comp, sleep,
  bone — not reproductive hormones. Community's most-loved real-world effect is **sleep**.
  IGF-1↑ = theoretical cancer caution.
- **Epitalon (C/D):** pineal/melatonin + telomerase. Sleep/circadian; telomerase→cancer caution.
- **Reality check:** actual perimenopause communities (r/Perimenopause) barely discuss peptides —
  they fix sleep/fatigue with basics (vit D, iron, ferritin, thyroid, exercise). Peptides here
  are still a biohacker niche; clinics already sell "perimenopause stacks" (marketing, not evidence).

## Retatrutide ("Reta")
- Triple agonist GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon. Weight loss (investigational, strong trials).
- Community pairs it mostly with **cagrilintide**, not KPV. GI side effects (nausea/constipation).

## Common stacks seen in community
- **KLOW** = GHK-Cu + KPV + BPC-157 + TB-500 (heal/anti-inflam). "First-week mild fatigue" noted.
- **GLOW** = GHK-Cu + BPC-157 + TB-500. KPV sometimes added and reportedly *reduced* injection welts.
- Philosophy practitioners preach: "start lean, add one biological key (e.g. KPV for
  inflammation) only when a specific job is failing" — not everything at once.
