# Stack & protocol builder reference

For building peptide protocols and combinations. **Everything here is community practice /
mechanistic reasoning, NOT validated regimens.** Research any specific pair live (`sonar-pro`)
before asserting synergy or safety. Always attach the safety layer and physician-oversight note.
Evidence tiers: A human-RCT · B human-small · C animal · D anecdotal.

## Known community stacks
- **KLOW** = GHK-Cu + KPV + BPC-157 + TB-500 → heal + anti-inflammation. "First-week mild fatigue" common.
- **GLOW** = GHK-Cu + BPC-157 + TB-500 → skin/recovery. Adding KPV reportedly *reduced* injection welts.
- **BPC-157 + TB-500** → the classic recovery pair (BPC local repair + TB-500 systemic).
- **CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin** → the classic GH pair (GHRH analog + GHRP secretagogue; synergistic pulse). PM.
- **Semax + Selank** → cognition + calm (focus without the jittery edge).
- **KPV + BPC-157** → most positive KPV gut/inflammation reports pair the two (rarely KPV alone).

## Synergy vs redundancy — think before stacking
- **Synergy** (different mechanisms, same goal): CJC-1295 (GHRH-R) + Ipamorelin (GHSR) → bigger GH pulse.
  BPC-157 (angiogenesis/local) + TB-500 (systemic actin/migration) → complementary repair.
- **Redundancy** (same lane, marginal add): stacking two GH secretagogues of the same class;
  two systemic anti-inflammatories with no site/route difference.
- **Route diversity beats piling on**: for gut, oral KPV + a systemic repair peptide covers more
  ground than two systemic anti-inflammatories.

## Timing rules that matter
- **Semax / stimulating peptides** → morning, by ~2–4 PM latest (insomnia risk). Never weekly (short t½).
- **GH pair (CJC/Ipa)** → before bed on empty stomach (GH pulses with sleep; food blunts it).
- **KPV subQ** → food timing irrelevant (systemic). KPV oral → empty stomach for systemic, with food for gut.
- **PT-141** → on-demand, ~45 min before, not chronic.
- **Weekly GLP peptides (Reta/Tirz)** → same day each week.

## Sequencing — "start lean"
Never launch a multi-peptide blend on day 1. Introduce **one peptide at a time**, ~a week apart,
so a reaction is attributable. Add a new "biological key" only when a specific job is failing.
Order: foundation/goal peptide first → recovery/support second → cosmetic/optimization last.

## Reconstitution & dosing math (U-100 insulin syringe)
- U-100: **100 units = 1 mL**, so **1 unit = 0.01 mL**. A "50-unit" syringe holds 0.5 mL.
- Concentration after mixing = vial_mg ÷ water_mL.
- **Units to draw = (dose_mg ÷ concentration_mg-per-mL) × 100**
  = (dose_mg × water_mL ÷ vial_mg) × 100.
- Worked example — 10 mg vial + **2 mL** bacteriostatic water = 5 mg/mL:
  - 0.5 mg dose → 0.1 mL → **10 units**. Whole vial = 40 units = 20 doses.
- **The concentration is UNDEFINED without the water volume** — always ask/state it. This is the
  #1 dosing error. Prefer 2 mL for a 10 mg vial so low doses land on clean unit marks and are
  accurate (1 mL makes a 0.5 mg dose only 5 units — hard to measure).
- Only **bacteriostatic** water; sterile technique; refrigerate reconstituted; rotate subQ sites
  (29–31G, 45–90°, no aspiration, inject slow).

## Post-dose symptom management (general info, not medical advice)
- Gas/bloating → **simethicone (Gas-X)** = safest, local, no meaningful interactions.
- IBS-type cramping → **peppermint oil (Colpermin)** helps spasm BUT worsens reflux/GERD.
- **AVOID NSAIDs (ibuprofen/Advil) for gut symptoms** — NSAIDs irritate the gut lining and can
  worsen inflammation / trigger IBD flares. Paracetamol/acetaminophen is gentler on the gut if a
  general analgesic is needed. Persistent/worsening symptoms, fever, blood, severe pain → physician.
