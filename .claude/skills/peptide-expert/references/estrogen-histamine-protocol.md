# Estrogen–histamine loop protocol (for Osher — PMDD + perimenopause + histamine/MCAS)

What women with the estrogen–histamine loop actually take. The loop: high/poorly-cleared estrogen →
more mast-cell histamine release + lower DAO → worse histamine symptoms, peaking late-luteal/premenstrual.
So you treat BOTH sides: help clear estrogen AND support histamine breakdown, pulsed around the cycle.
Evidence tiers: A human-RCT / B human-small or strong-mechanism / C mechanistic/functional-practice.

## A) ESTROGEN-clearance side (the piece the histamine toolkit was missing)
| Supp | Role | Brands | Dose | Note for her |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Calcium-D-Glucarate** ⭐ start here | phase-II glucuronidation, inhibits β-glucuronidase → estrogen stays bound & excreted | Thorne, Pure Encapsulations, NOW | 500–1000 mg/day split w/ food | gentlest estrogen-side option; usually clean capsule [C] |
| **DIM** (diindolylmethane) | shifts estrogen to safer 2-OH metabolites; mild estrogen-lowering | Thorne DIM Advantage, Seeking Health DIM | 100–200 mg AM w/ food (start 50–100) | ⚠️ can over-lower estrogen → worsen PMDD mood/anxiety in perimenopause; mildly lowers BP. Ideally check estrogen labs first, start low. [C] |
| **Sulforaphane** (broccoli sprout) | Nrf2 detox + estrogen metabolism | Thorne Crucera, Jarrow Broccomax, Avmacol | 30–60 mg w/ food | brassica → can cause GI gas/flare in sensitive MCAS; start low [C] |
| Choline / phosphatidylcholine, milk thistle, NAC | bile flow / liver estrogen clearance | — | — | secondary [C] |

## B) HISTAMINE side — the two that matter most for HER (work on both sides of the loop)
- **Vitamin B6 / P5P** ⭐⭐ — DAO cofactor (breaks down histamine) **AND** needed for progesterone synthesis +
  PMDD mood. The perfect dual-purpose supp for the loop. Brands: Pure Encapsulations P5P, Seeking Health P5P.
  Dose 25–50 mg, esp. luteal half. Keep <100 mg chronic (neuropathy). [B mechanism]
- **Zinc** — DAO cofactor + supports progesterone. Thorne Zinc Picolinate 10–30 mg w/ food. [C]
- (Plus her existing core: quercetin, clean vitamin C, magnesium glycinate, DAO — see iherb-clean-shopping-list.md.)

## C) PROGESTERONE / cycle side (progesterone is mast-cell stabilizing)
- Magnesium glycinate + B6 + omega-3 (Nordic Naturals) + vitamin E + zinc = classic PMS/PMDD set, increased luteal.
- **Vitex (chasteberry)** ⚠️ — raises luteal progesterone, RCT support for PMS, but **mixed/controversial in PMDD**
  (helps some, worsens mood in others). For her: very cautious or skip, only with cycle-tracking + oversight.
  Capsules not tinctures (alcohol = histamine). [B for PMS, mixed PMDD]
- Bioidentical progesterone (luteal) — strongly mast-cell-stabilizing + PMDD; prescription/monitored.

## D) METHYLATION → HNMT (the second histamine-clearing enzyme)
Histamine is also cleared by HNMT, which depends on methylation. **B12 + folate** support it — and ties to her
**low folate (3.6)**. Community repeatedly flagged **B12 deficiency as a hidden root cause** → worth testing B12 + iron.
Use hydroxo-B12 + low-dose methylfolate (Seeking Health / Thorne); don't push high methyl doses (anxiety/insomnia
in sensitive nervous systems). [C]

## The core women actually take (daily, ramped in luteal phase)
1. Magnesium glycinate (night) · 2. Clean vitamin C 500–1000mg · 3. B6/P5P 25–50mg · 4. Quercetin (±luteolin) ·
5. Methylated B-complex (B6/B12/folate) · + DAO before trigger meals · Zinc · Omega-3.
Estrogen side: Calcium-D-Glucarate first → DIM cautiously (labs) later.

## Perimenstrual flare (days ~21–28) — what helped today's kind of episode
Ramp quercetin (2–3×/day) the week before · magnesium glycinate nightly · extra vitamin C the week before ·
DAO before meals those days · evening B6 (DAO + progesterone) · low-histamine diet in luteal phase.

## Caution flags specific to her
DIM (over-lowers estrogen / PMDD mood / mild BP drop) · Vitex (mixed in PMDD) · high-dose B6 chronic (neuropathy) ·
aggressive methylation (agitation) · flavored/effervescent C or Mg (citric acid) · flavored fish oil · sulforaphane
(GI flare). Everything supportive, not validated; HI/MCAS is individual — track per cycle.
