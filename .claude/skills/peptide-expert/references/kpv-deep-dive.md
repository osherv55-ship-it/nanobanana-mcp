# KPV — deep mechanism & evidence reference

Distilled from a sonar-deep-research run (primary literature: Dalmasso/Merlin/Sitaraman,
Land, Getting, Kannengiesser, Bettenworth, Luger, Böhm, Cutuli, Songok). Evidence is
**preclinical**; no human RCTs. Tiers: C = animal/cell, D = anecdotal.

## Origin — α-MSH's anti-inflammatory heritage in 3 amino acids
KPV = Lys-Pro-Val = C-terminal tripeptide of α-MSH (residues 11-13). Retains most of α-MSH's
anti-inflammatory activity **without** the pigmentary/endocrine effects, because it lacks the
His-Phe-Arg-Trp melanocortin pharmacophore → very low MC-receptor affinity, no melanogenesis.
"Won't make you tan" is mechanistically correct.

## Mechanism — two stages
1. **PepT1 "self-targeting" (fast uptake):** KPV enters cells via the H+-coupled di/tripeptide
   transporter PepT1. PepT1 is minimal in healthy colon but **upregulated in inflamed tissue** →
   KPV is preferentially imported exactly where inflammation is. Knock down PepT1 → KPV stops
   working (Dalmasso). Feedback loop: more inflammation → more PepT1 → more KPV uptake → less
   inflammation.
2. **Nuclear-transport blockade (the elegant part):** inside the cell KPV goes to the nucleus,
   binds **importin-α3** (armadillo domains 7-8) and competitively blocks p65RelA (NF-κB) from
   being trafficked in; stabilizes IκBα. Result: NF-κB trapped in cytoplasm → ↓ transcription of
   TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-8, eotaxin, MMP-9, adhesion molecules. Also inhibits p38 MAPK. This is
   distinct from typical NF-κB inhibitors (which hit upstream IKK/proteasome). Open question: is
   the importin blockade specific to NF-κB or does it perturb other transcription factors? (safety
   unknown).
3. **Rapid signaling layer (explains the acute felt effects):** MC1R→cAMP begins in seconds-minutes,
   and cAMP→PKA→eNOS→NO→vasodilation → warmth/flushing/tingling. This is the fast peripheral
   response; the anti-inflammatory genomic effect is the slow one (hours-days). KPV does NOT cross
   an intact blood-brain barrier (~5-10% only if BBB compromised) → any "vitality/mood" is
   peripheral/autonomic, not central.

## Receptor debate — settled
KPV's anti-inflammatory action is **MC1R-independent**: retained in MC1R-nonfunctional (e/e) mice
(rescued them from DSS colitis), not blocked by SHU9119, doesn't raise macrophage cAMP like α-MSH.
So it's intracellular/transporter-driven, not classic melanocortin-receptor pharmacology.

## Preclinical models
- **Gut/IBD — strongest:** effective across 4 colitis models (DSS, TNBS, CD45RB transfer, IL-10 KO);
  ↑survival, ↓histology score, ↓MPO, ↓cytokines. Anchor dose: **oral 2.5 mg/kg in mice** (Dalmasso
  2008); human-equivalent extrapolated ~0.2 mg (NOT tested).
- **Skin:** contact dermatitis, wound healing, keratinocyte protection from pollution (↓IL-1β);
  patent literature proposes it as a steroid alternative (no skin thinning). Less developed than gut.
- **Airway:** in bronchial epithelium ↓IL-8, ↓eotaxin, ↓MMP-9 via the same nuclear-import block
  (Land). No in-vivo asthma model tested — cells only.
- **Antimicrobial — CONFLICTED:** early reports of anti-Staph/anti-Candida activity; later work
  (Songok) failed to replicate (MIC >100 µM). Unreliable claim.
- **Cancer/other:** speculative only.

## Delivery science
Oral works because KPV is small + PepT1-transported (survives partial digestion) → the only route
with a real gut-targeting mechanism. Advanced: **HA-KPV nanoparticles / hydrogels** target inflamed
colon (CD44 + PepT1). Topical for local skin. SubQ = systemic (site doesn't gut-target).

## The evidence gap (what's missing)
- **Zero human RCTs.** No Phase I-III, no human PK, no dose-finding, no formal safety profile.
- Toxicology: short-term animal only. No genotox/carcinogenicity/reproductive.
- **FDA Category 2** bulk substance (insufficient safety/efficacy evidence for compounding).
- Validation would need: GMP production → 2-species tox → Phase I safety → Phase II efficacy
  (UC/Crohn's/psoriasis) → Phase III vs standard. None done.

## Honest bottom line
Preclinical case is **scientifically strong and mechanistically elegant** (PepT1 self-targeting +
importin/NF-κB blockade, consistent across barrier tissues). Clinical case is **essentially untested**
in humans. Correct stance: informed optimism + scientific humility. Biggest unknowns: long-term
safety, efficacy vs/with existing drugs, effect on human immune system & microbiome.
