## His verified teachings (attributable from show notes/transcripts)

### Peptides he recommends, by goal
- **Fat loss / body comp / GH axis:** **Tesamorelin, CJC-1295, Ipamorelin** (GH secretagogues).
- **Healing / gut / injury:** **BPC-157** (his personal recovery story centerpiece).
- **Longevity:** **Epitalon (Epithalon)**.
- **Mitochondria / energy:** **SS-31**, **NAD+**.
- **Cognition / brain (personal use):** **Cerebrolysin, C-Max (Cortexin-related), Selank**;
  mentions bioregulator protocols framed around Alzheimer's/Parkinson's.
- **Bioregulators:** short peptides for **thyroid** (claims possible reduced med reliance) and
  **autoimmune** (endometriosis) — also discusses LDN (a drug, not a peptide) in that context.
- **GLP-1s (Ozempic/Mounjaro class):** for obesity; his framing: they cut **cravings / "food noise"**
  more than appetite, and he advocates **microdosing** as safer/more effective than aggressive dosing.

### Cycling philosophy (his signature theme)
- "More is not better — cycling and dosing matter." Peptides **optimize existing biology, don't replace it.**
- Concrete structures he states (Halftime Show): some peptides **BID, 5 days on / 2 off**; others
  **daily for a 6-week cycle**, then a break — "compounded effect over time, then take a break."
- General protocol (weight-loss episode): a **custom blend for 3–6 weeks → break → repeat a few times/year**,
  individualized. Claims correctly-prescribed, cycled use is largely side-effect-free.
- **Anti-cookie-cutter:** biological individuality > one-size-fits-all protocols. Runs multi-peptide
  stacks himself (mentioned a **5-peptide travel body-composition stack** — names/doses not public).

### "Big mistakes" he warns about
- No cycling / continuous high-dose use; "more is better" thinking.
- Cookie-cutter protocols ignoring the individual.
- GLP-1 misuse/overuse (high doses, misunderstanding the craving-reduction mechanism).
- Using peptides as a **substitute** for sleep/stress/nutrition foundations instead of on top of them.

### Safety / sourcing
- Frames peptides as favorable-safety **when properly prescribed and cycled**.
- Post-FDA-change landscape discussion; his company background implies advocacy for
  quality-controlled/lab-tested sourcing over grey-market (inference from role, not an explicit quote).
