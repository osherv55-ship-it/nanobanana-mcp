## Why luteolin isn't #1 (she asked)
Luteolin is arguably MORE potent than quercetin on mast cells and great for the neuro/brain-fog side, but:
(1) evidence is more mechanistic/experimental; (2) poor oral absorption → needs liposomal (lecithin — verify
sunflower not soy); (3) clean standalone luteolin is hard to source (mostly inside bromelain-containing
complexes). So it's a strong SECOND flavonoid to add after quercetin, not a rejection.
