# Stack architecture — branched, phased & sequenced combination protocols

> **What this file is for.** Trevor and his ecosystem rarely give a flat list. They give an *architecture*:
> compounds assigned to failures, sequenced, cycled on/off, with carbohydrate phasing, a cofactor layer, and
> a tolerance/reactivity plan. This file lets the agent reproduce that **combination architecture** — the part
> the user actually wants — while holding the dosing boundary: **no doses, routes, frequencies, cycles-as-numbers,
> or reconstitution for any peptide, research compound, prescription drug or hormone.** "Which compounds, in what
> arrangement, and why" = yes. "How many mg, how often" = a physician with labs in hand.

## The architecture primitives (express these, without numbers)

1. **Failure-targeting.** Assign every compound to one of the three failures and say why, in one mechanism line.
   - 🔥 inflammation / tolerance: BPC-157, KPV, LL-37, Thymosin α1, GHK-Cu, omega-3, curcumin, PEA.
   - ⛽ insulin resistance: MOTS-c, berberine/dihydroberberine, ALA, GLP-1-class (Rx), chromium.
   - 🔋 ATP / mitochondria: SS-31, NAD+ (and NMN/NR oral), PQQ, L-carnitine, CoQ10/ubiquinol, creatine, methylene blue.
2. **Sequencing.** Some stacks assert an order (e.g. "MOTS-c before SS-31"). State the order *as his claim* and
   flag it: **no human study has tested peptide sequencing** `[D / his framing]`. Order is a hypothesis, not a rule.
3. **On/off cycling.** GH secretagogues, NAD+, and GH-axis peptides are usually pulsed (on blocks, then off blocks).
   The *principle* (receptors desensitize, so you pulse) is real pharmacology; the *specific week counts* are not
   validated in humans and are not reproduced here.
4. **Phased carbohydrate modulation.** "Prime with carbs → introduce the metabolic compound → taper carbs" is the
   recurring shape. The rationale (don't start a fat-oxidation push from an energy deficit; carbs support NAD/AMPK
   adaptation) is a reasonable teaching model `[his framing + literature on substrate availability]`.
5. **Cofactor layer (this is layer 1, supplements — doses allowed, within UL).** The engine needs its cofactors:
   methylated B-complex, magnesium glycinate, ubiquinol, selenium, zinc (↔copper balance), iron if ferritin low,
   PQQ, L-carnitine as the fatty-acid shuttle. Without these the peptides "run on half power" — and unlike the
   peptides, these have doses and grades (see `06-labs.md` and `03b`).
6. **Tolerance / reactivity management.** A compound can provoke a reaction (MOTS-c histamine/mast-cell reactivity
   is the common one). The mechanism: a leaky gut barrier + high histamine tone amplifies the response. The honest
   handling is in the next section — **not** a self-administered antihistamine-dosing manual.

## Reactivity & tolerance module (mechanism, not a dosing drill)

- **What it is.** Some people flush, feel wired/anxious, headache, or crash on MOTS-c. Mechanistically this reads
  as a histamine / mast-cell response, often on a background of gut-barrier permeability, not "the peptide working".
- **The real-world moves, framed honestly:**
  - Stop the compound. A reaction is a stop sign, not a dose-down signal.
  - H1/H2 support and a low-histamine window exist as a concept — but which agent, how much, and whether it is safe
    *with her atomoxetine and her other meds* is a physician/pharmacist call. The agent names the concept and routes
    the numbers to a clinician. It does **not** hand out an OTC antihistamine regimen as a protocol.
  - The gut-barrier angle (barrier repair + removing histamine load) is a legitimate medium-term direction; the
    specific multi-peptide "gut repair stack" circulating for this is **D-level and commercial** (see integrity check).
  - **Non-peptide metabolic alternative.** When a peptide is not tolerated, the same failure can be approached with
    evidence-graded, non-injectable levers: ALA and dihydroberberine (⛽, grade B surrogates), L-carnitine (🔋, C),
    plus the foundations. This is often the *better* answer for someone who reacts, and it carries real human doses.

## Evidence framing for any branched protocol

Mechanistic elegance is not human evidence. Every multi-peptide branched/phased protocol in this ecosystem is, for
humans, **grade D** (mouse or none): the sequencing is untested, the phasing is untested, and the "studies" offered
to support them are usually mouse-framed marketing. Say this plainly when presenting an architecture. The parts that
carry real human grades are the supplements, the GLP-1-class drugs (Rx), the foundations, and the lab monitoring.

## 🔎 Integrity check — how to read a "protocol paper" before trusting it

Treat a document as **commercial marketing, grade D, and never a dosing source** if any of these are true (the two
PDFs uploaded 2026-10 hit all of them):
- It carries a store or coaching CTA (e.g. "GET EVERYTHING HERE", a shop link, "work with Dr …", a Black Card pitch).
- It presents a **mouse model** (C57BL/6, DSS, high-fat-diet) but lists **human injection doses** to "follow exactly,
  no alteration".
- No author, no DOI, no journal, no raw data; printed from a phone; editing artifacts in the text.
- Uniformly perfect results (e.g. "all p<0.001, 100% survival, no toxicity") with an IACUC number as the only
  credibility anchor.
These can inform the *architecture* (sequencing, phasing, cofactors) as a hypothesis to teach — never the doses, and
never cited as evidence. If the agent uses such a document, it must label it: "commercial/mouse-framed, grade D".

## Hard line (unchanged)

The agent reproduces the *shape* of these protocols — which compounds, which failure, what order and phasing, which
cofactors, how to handle a reaction — and refuses the *numbers* for peptides/research compounds/Rx/hormones. Supplement
and foundation numbers are allowed within the upper intake level, each with a grade. For a specific person who is
self-administering, the agent's job is honest mechanism + "take this architecture and the labs to a physician", not a
turnkey injection schedule.
